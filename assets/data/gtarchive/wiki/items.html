Nyan Hat - Taco Dog

Nyan Hat
1 could be obtained from purchasing the item itself for 25,000 Gems in the Growtopia Store.
ItemID: 574
"NYAN NYAN NYAN NYAN NYAN NYAN NYAN NYAN NYAN"

The Nyan Hat was a hat item which was added alongside Phonecats Hat on March 7, 2013. Both items were based on the Nyan Cat internet meme.

In May 2013, Christopher Torres, the copyright owner of Nyan Cat, sued Warner Bros. for an unauthorized usage of the character.[2] In light of this, the developers decided to alter the Nyan Hat's design in order to avoid any copyright issue themselves.

Taco Dog
1 could be obtained from purchasing the item itself for 25,000 Gems in the Growtopia Store.
ItemID: 574
"Taco Dog would never sue anybody, he just likes delicious tacos."

Per fan suggestion, the Taco Dog was introduced on May 11, 2013 as the Nyan Hat's replacement. While the Taco Dog seemed like a good idea at that time, it was soon discovered that the Taco Dog was another creation of Torres.[3] A poll was soon posted, asking the playerbase for ideas for the second redesign.[4]

The possible choices for the redesign at that time were Yerfdog, Piggy Bank, Turtle, Kitty, and Puppy. The poll closed on May 13, with Turtle winning 45.12% of the votes. The Turtle Hat was then rolled out on May 16. A teaser was first posted on the RTsoft forums hours before its release.[5]

A Burglar

A Burglar
Unobtainable.
ItemID: 784
In-game description is not available.

A Burglar was a solid SFX foreground block which was added as part of the GROWTECHPHARMA world event on May 16, 2013, a day before the addition of Zombie Defense Pack. When a player punched the block, it played a hurt animation and said, "Ow, Stop!".

The item was completely overwritten by Tombstone, along with its data and sprite. Its sound effect is now played only when an Awkward Friendly Unicorn is punched.

World Cup Winner's Jersey

World Cup Winner's Jersey
Purchase the Sportsball Pack for 20,000 in the Growtopia Store.
ItemID: 6676
"To make this jersey, we had to steal a sports almanac from the distant future and used it to find the winners of this year's World Cup! However, to prevent a paradox, we're keeping it in this time-locked box until the results are official!"

The World Cup Winner's Jersey 2018 is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of the Startopia update on July 1, 2018. Before France was announced as the winner of the 2018 World Cup, the developers added this item as a component item under the name of "World Cup Winner Jersey". A sprite of a black-and-white box was used as the item's icon.

The item was overwritten by World Cup Winner's Jersey 2018, along with its data and sprite.
