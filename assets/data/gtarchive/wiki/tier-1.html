Monthly Event Days

  • Carnival : Once a month, on a random weekend or before weekend (Friday)
  • Voucher Dayz : Once a month, on a random weekend or 2-1 days before weekend
  • Night of the Comet : Once a month, on the 28th day of every month
  • Locke The Traveling Salesman : Twice a month, once during the first half of the month, and another at the second half
  • The Grand Tournament : Once a month, on the 7th of every month
  • Surgery Day/Geiger Day/Ghost Day/All Howl's Eve/Mutant Kitchen : Once a month

RTsoft

RTsoft aka Robinson Technologies is a video game developer located in Japan and founded by Seth Robinson. The company produced the BBS door games Legend of the Red Dragon, Planets: The Exploration of Space and Growtopia, an experimental multiplayer creative sandbox created as a collaboration with Hamumu Software, released in 2013 for iOS, Android, Microsoft Windows, and macOS.

Roots

Roots & Mature Roots may refer to:

  • Roots, an update released in July 2019.
  • Roots, a block created from a Fertile Soil Block when a particular bountiful item's tree has finished growing on top of it.
  • Mature Roots, a block created from a Roots block when a particular bountiful item's tree has finished growing on top of it for the fourth time.
