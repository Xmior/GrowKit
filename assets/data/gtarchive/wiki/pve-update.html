Rollback VI : This occurred when new code was implemented that would permit for a future update, aka PvE update. However, this code caused issues, leading some players to lose progress and the servers were quickly placed into maintenance. The issue with the code was fixed.

Community Update 2021 #2 : We can confirm that the new PvE-mode was leaked due to data mining. It is well known at this point that we were working on a new game mode altogether. The fact of the matter is that this new game mode is simply not up to the standard in its current state, which is why we never wanted to properly announce it. The development is still ongoing, but, as before, we do not want to promise a release date until we feel fully confident to present it to you.

Community Update 2022 #1 : As previously mentioned, the team is still continuously working on the game mode, and we are trying our best to bring it up to the expected standards that will be enjoyed and appreciated by the whole community. This is why, unfortunately, there isn’t a confirmed/fixed date on when it will be released.

PVE Statement 2022 : We wanted to come out and make a statement on a topic which has popped back up during PAW '22, and one which has periodically been a discussion point for the community in the last couple of years, PVE. We feel transparency here is important and wanted to clearly state that we don’t plan to release PVE. As many of you know, PVE was a new game mode which was being worked on by us but was never released. We’ve previously touched on why, and that is that we’re not happy releasing a feature this big which isn’t a stable gameplay experience for you all, and as the development went on, we made the decision to shelve the work in favor of other features. Of course, we also learned a lot during the development of PVE and apply these learnings for new and exciting features we’ve made and continue to work on. There are bits and pieces of the PVE feature that we’ve used in features since that initial development, with a recent example being the story elements of our Star Trek: Prodigy event. As a topic, PVE may be something we might tackle later, but not in the form we originally envisioned, but instead, as a completely new feature. However, we’re not currently working or planning this. We know many were excited for PVE’s release, which is why we felt it was important to address and close this topic, rather than keeping you all in suspense.

Community Update 2022 #2 : Now let's talk about PVE and its cancellation, the topic that has dominated conversation for a while. As mentioned in our first announcement regarding this topic, we opted to clearly outline to the community in the interests of transparency, that PVE is not a feature we are actively working on or intending to release. We are aware that this wasn’t the news many wanted to hear, but we feel it was important to have everyone on the same page regarding it.

How does PvE Mode work? (Theory)

  PvE Mode is very similar to Startopia 2.0 and Growlactus, there are “tiers” like “Mk. I” in this mode, and when you pass enough paths, “Boss Switch” will welcome you. You can fight the boss with “Boss Switch” or continue your normal adventure. By the way, they told us, “There are bits and pieces of the PVE feature that we’ve used in features since that initial development, with a recent example being the story elements of our Star Trek: Prodigy event,” so it was probably going to be a tutorial place like the “Star Trek: Prodigy” event where a person will teach us the game mode, and we will start going on adventures. By the way, there is an NPC (Dungeon Master), so this theory is very plausible. Also in the “Startrek: Prodigy” event, we were fighting an NPC (kinda), attacking him with the “Gigablaster” consumable, so we still got a taste of PvE.  

You're probably going to complete five quests, like Startopia, before you hit the boss level, which is kinda like Growlactus.

Theories

The Dungeons update was supposed to be a Startopia 2.0 update (hint: some boss fights, etc.), but they left aside the boss concept, etc. After a decision, they were going to make an update about bosses and NPCs. They started working on it, but canceled it and used items from other updates with some changes. They probably tried to do it again, then added PvE level, etc., and new items. After 2 years of working on PvE, but no serious work because they weren't planning to share it, and they don't share it.
