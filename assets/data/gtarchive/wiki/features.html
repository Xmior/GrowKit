The added & removed Features to Growtopia

Operating Systems

WebOS, Xbox One, Nintendo Switch & Playstation 4 is removed operating systems from Growtopia.[1][2]

Removed Daily Challenges

Some Daily Challenges have received many player complaints about abuse of the challenge by other players. As such, they have been removed and their special prizes were given new recipes.

Bunny Battle: Collect naturally spawned Magic Eggs for points. Removed due to players exploiting the challenge by hoarding them prior to the event and cycling popular worlds filled with Wooden Platform to maximise chance to spawn.

+1pts Collect a naturally spawned Magic Egg.

Zombie Stomp: Kill players who have been infected with the g-Virus during the Pandemic for points. Removed due to players simply exploiting the Broadcasting system to bring infected players into their world.

+1pts Per zombie killed.

Cosmic Crown: Collect naturally spawned Comet Dust and Antimatter Dust for points. Only appears during the Night Of The Comet event. Removed due to players exploiting the challenge by hoarding them prior to the event and cycling popular worlds filled with Wooden Platform to maximise chance to spawn.

+1pts Collect a naturally spawned Comet Dust.
+20pts Collect a naturally spawned Antimatter Dust.

Super-Block Event

Main article: Super-Block Event

The Super-Block Event was a removed Grow Event added as part of the Guilds update on April 1, 2018. It began on April 13, 2018, a day after Easter Week had concluded, and ended on April 16, 2018, thereby lasting for 4 days in total. This was the first and only occurrence of the event.

The Super-Block Event was the first ever guild event added to the game, and is considered a precursor to the current version of Seasonal Clashes.

Trade-Scan & Trade-Scan Credits

The Trade-Scan & Trade-Scan Credits is an unobtainable component which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week Day 3 on August 30, 2023. This item serves as an image placeholder for the trade-scan tab found when doing the command /tradescan or by wrenching oneself. A player may exchange a World Lock for five Trade-Scan Credit which allows the player to view the average price of five items.

Ubisoft Club Rewards

Main article: Ubisoft Club Rewards

The Ubisoft Club Rewards system was one of the few features available only to the discontinued console edition of Growtopia. By earning achievements, players could obtain exclusive tokens, which would be able to be traded for unique items.

Four of the exclusive items obtained through this system has been added to the PC/mobile version of Growtopia: Glowing Shirt, Plunger, The Brother Hood, and the Unicorn. One, however, is still yet to be added: the Energy Drink.
