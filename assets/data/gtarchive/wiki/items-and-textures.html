Phonecats Hat

The Phonecats Hat is an unobtainable hat item which was added on March 7, 2013, exclusively given to Phonecats/pcats (YouTube: phonecatss) for his contributions as a content creator during Growtopia's early stages.[1]

Phonecats Hat sprite comparison

Its old design was similar to the Nyan Hat, both of which were based on the Nyan Cat internet meme. The item's sprite was changed sometime between SummerFest 2013 and Rise of the Desert Dragons (a few months after Nyan Hat was replaced by Turtle Hat) in order to prevent any legal issues.[2][3]

Emerald Lock

The Emerald Lock is a solid lock foreground block which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2015 on March 13, 2015. It gives an effect akin to that of Arroz Con Pollo, giving a 10% chance of adding an extra gem drop when breaking blocks.

On April 2, 2016, hours after the Artsy April update was launched, the texture of the Emerald Lock was changed. 4 dark green dots were added on both the left and right side of the lock, seemingly mimicking a four-leaf clover, to prevent players from painting regular World Locks to make them look like an Emerald Lock.

War Balloons and Bucket of War Balloons

The War Balloons and Buckets of War Balloons are unsplicable consumables which were added as part of Balloon Warz 2017. There are 3 War Balloon variants ( Small, Medium, and Large), as well as 3 Bucket of War Balloons variants ( Small, Medium, and Large).

Ever since Brr-lloon Warz 2017, the Balloon Warz event has always been carried out around winter. Subsequently, the sprites of all War Balloon and Bucket of War Balloons items have been permanently redesigned to appear as if they have been covered by snow and ice.

Riding Turkey

The Riding Turkey is an unsplicable feet item that was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2018 on November 22, 2018.

When it was first added, the Riding Turkey had a different inventory icon, presumably based on an early design of the item.[4] By December 2018, the sprite has been changed to the one currently used.

Black Cat Ears

The Black Cat Ears are an unsplicable item that was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2019 Day 3 on August 21, 2019.

An update on August 23, 2019 saw a redesign to the Black Cat Ears. JamSandwich, who worked as part of the developer team, pushed for this change after reviewing the playerbase's reaction towards the previous design.[5] The old sprites could be found in player_cosmetics1.rttex and player_cosmetics1_icon.rttex, and no longer exist in the current versions of the files.
