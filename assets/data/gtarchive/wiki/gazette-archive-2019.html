February 15th: Rollback V

Hello Growtopians,

As you might know, we have performed a rollback in order to make the game available again. Unfortunately, it was the only option due to a large-scale, cascading disk failure.

We have restored your accounts to the 12th of February (approximately 15:00 Growtopia Time), which implies that, sadly, all the progress done after the mentioned date has been lost.
However, your IAP won't be lost and those gems will be re-added to your account - in fact, they will be doubled as compensation!

We have also started Apology Days to help you catch up on the progress lost:

Increased changes of getting gems and seeds while smashing blocks;

IAPs will give 25% more gems;

The Rollback Plaque V will be available in the game for a limited time.

Valentines event will continue for three extra days AFTER the Apology event is over.
