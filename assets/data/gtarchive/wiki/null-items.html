Console Launch Items

These items were featured in the console versions of Growtopia and were never added to the regular version.

7-Day VIP Token

7-Day VIP Token
Buy from shop for 2,99$
ItemID is currently unknown.
"Consume this for seven full days of extra-special treatment! You'll get access to unique skins, a chance of doubling any XP earned, a growth time reduction for your trees, and every day upon login you will receive 20 season tokens (as long as there's a seasonal clash running) and 500 gems!"

The 7-Day VIP Token is an unsplicable consuable which was added as part of the Console Launch at 18 July, 2019.

Energy Drink

Energy Drink
Redeem for 20 in the Ubisoft Club Rewards.
ItemID is currently unknown.
"Full of completely harmless and almost certainly all-natural chemicals guaranteed by totally-trustworthy GrowTech engineers to sharpen your wits! Grants a 30% bonus to all XP gained for 5 whole days after you drink it!"

The Energy Drink was a consumable redeemable as a rare Ubisoft Club Reward.

When consumed, it granted the XP Buff: Energy Drinks mod to the player, which gave a 30% bonus to all XP gained by the player for 5 days.

Growtoken

"You can earn these from Crazy Jim and Sales-Man - try dialing 12345 or 53785 on your Telephone for details! Spend them in the store on special items! If you have 100 Growtokens you can tap them in your inventory to combine them into a Mega Growtoken!"
